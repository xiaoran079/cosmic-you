Everyone has a type. Whether you're looking for everyone's favorite frat boy, a funny nerd, a beautiful and mysterious person, or someone who just likes to eat food with you, when you realize someone has these qualities, you can't help but start building a future for the two of you in your head. But have you ever gotten frustrated when the qualities you look for in a person don't seem to lead to your happily ever after? You might even start wondering whether your own preferences are working against you. 

Maybe it's because you're relying on only what you know about your star sign. You've gone your whole life knowing you're a Scorpio, an Aries, or a Taurus, so naturally, you look for a Cancer who can have deep conversations with you, a Sagittarius to go on adventures with, or a Virgo who can go toe-to-toe with you when analyzing why your friends were acting weird last night. 

However, your Sun sign is only one piece of the picture. Your birth chart as a whole offers a more well-rounded look at your strengths, weaknesses, emotional needs, desires, and other personality traits. This means that even if your Sun sign is considered compatible with someone else's, other placements in their chart could introduce some unexpected friction. For example, Scorpio and Cancer are traditionally considered a highly compatible match. But what if that Cancer has an Aquarius Moon? While their Cancer Sun might offer the emotional depth and sensitivity a Scorpio is drawn to, their Aquarius Moon might make them seem more emotionally detached or independent than the Scorpio is comfortable with. Suddenly, that seemingly perfect zodiac match might not be so straightforward. 

If your Sun sign only tells part of the story, what other placements should you be paying attention to when it comes to attraction? Start with Venus, Mars, and Jupiter. Venus reveals what you find attractive, how you express affection, and what makes you feel appreciated in a relationship. Basically, Venus is your standards and what you need to allow someone into your romantic life. Mars offers insight into the qualities that spark your desire, the way you pursue someone, and the kind of chemistry you gravitate toward. Maybe Mars can help you find your flirting style. Jupiter, on the other hand, is associated with growth, values, and the qualities you admire in a long-term partner. In some traditional astrological interpretations, Jupiter is also used to explore the qualities a woman may look for in a husband. Jupiter asks you to sit down and be real with yourself, what is it that you need for a happy and successful romantic life, long term? Rather than focusing on just one placement, looking at these planets together can help you better understand what catches your attention, what keeps you interested, and what you may actually need from a partner. You might discover that the qualities you're initially attracted to aren't necessarily the same qualities that make you feel fulfilled in a relationship. And that's worth knowing before you start planning that future together! 

Ultimately, your ideal type and your astrological compatibility don't have to be at odds. Knowing what you're drawn to can help you understand your preferences, while exploring your birth chart can introduce new questions about your relationship patterns and connections with others. Astrology isn't a guarantee that two people will work out, but it can be a fun starting point for self and relational discovery.
