A career is more than a title on a business card. It's also the way you solve problems, collaborate, and choose what matters.

## What lights you up?

Fire signs are traditionally associated with initiative; earth signs with consistency; air signs with ideas; and water signs with intuition. These are creative archetypes, not hiring recommendations.

Instead of asking *Which job is my sign destined for?*, ask: **When do I feel most curious and capable?**

## Try a strengths inventory

Write down three moments when you felt proud of your work or studies. What skills were you using? What kind of support helped? Those concrete examples are much more useful for career decisions than a zodiac label alone.

## Your next move

Choose one skill to practice this month, and find a small project where you can test it.
