Sometimes a conversation flows so easily that you lose track of time. Other times, even a small misunderstanding feels impossible to untangle.

## The spark

Astrology offers playful language for different styles of affection. Venus is often linked to attraction, while the Moon is associated with emotional comfort.

But no chart can measure love. **Compatibility is something people build**, through honesty, curiosity, and the ability to repair a difficult moment.

## A question worth asking

What makes you feel genuinely understood? Is it someone remembering tiny details, cheering on your ambitions, or giving you space when you need it?

## Make it real

Share your answer with someone you care about. The best surprise might not come from the stars, but from what you learn about each other.
