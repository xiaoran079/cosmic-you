Your Sun sign might be the part of astrology you know best. But your **Moon sign** invites a different question: *What makes you feel at home with yourself?*

## Your emotional weather

In astrology, the Moon is associated with instinct, comfort, and emotional needs. Rather than treating it as a fixed personality label, try using it as a prompt to notice your patterns.

When you feel overwhelmed, do you seek a quiet room, a familiar friend, or a new adventure? Your answer may tell you more about your needs than any horoscope can.

## A small experiment

For one week, jot down what restores your energy after a difficult day. Look for the people, places, and routines that consistently help.

Your birth chart can be a conversation starter. **Your lived experience gets the final word.**
